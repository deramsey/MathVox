All keyboard shortcuts

Every action in the field works from the keyboard, including the on-screen math keyboard and the options menu:

  • Shift+Alt+Space (Shift+Option+Space on Mac) — show or hide the on-screen math keyboard
  • Alt+Space (Option+Space on Mac) — open the field's options menu
  • Tab / Shift+Tab — jump to the next/previous placeholder (e.g. into or out of a fraction)
  • Arrow keys — move through the expression; Space moves out of a group (like out of an exponent)
  • / for a fraction, ^ for an exponent, _ for a subscript, or type a backslash (\) to enter raw LaTeX at the cursor
  • Alt+Ctrl+↓ — have the whole expression read aloud from inside the field (same as Listen below)

Example: e=mc^2. Handy for pasting LaTeX or fixing braces. Select Convert (or press Ctrl+Enter) to apply it.

Draw with a mouse, pen or finger. When you pause, MathVox shows what it read so you can check it, then Insert into Equation adds it at the cursor. Drawing needs a pointer; with a keyboard, type in the field above or use the LaTeX tab.
